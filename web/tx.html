Works with the HP 48S/SX, 48G/GX and 49G on a serial cable. Start the calculator's server, connect, and its variables show here: get them as files, put files onto it.

This browser cannot reach a serial port. For a calculator on a cable, open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. The emulated calculator works here too.

Which calculator is on the cable?

Trouble with the cable?
  • The calculator needs its own serial cable, and most computers a USB serial adapter for it.
  • On Linux, your user must be in the dialout group (uucp on Arch) to reach the port.
  • On Windows, adapters with fake Prolific chips are refused by current drivers; one with an FTDI or a genuine chip works.
  • The page finds the speed by itself. If that fails, set a fixed one in the settings.
